## Findings (root causes, both since initial commit 7d574d2)

1. `src/game/art/rig.ts` `limbPath`/`chainPath` traced their two sides crossed
   (arc ends at a - n*w/2; path went to b + n*w/2), producing bow-tie paths
   whose middles the nonzero fill left empty. Probe: 60->40 limb middle column
   0 px filled (after fix 50); bent chain 19/40/20 (after 46/40/36). Every
   in-play brow, mouth and nose is a limb path - hence the broken "comma"
   features - and torsos, limbs, sashes, belts, hafts and the horse were hollow.
2. `src/game/art/portrait.ts` gradients faded to `rgba(0,0,0,0)`; canvas
   interpolates unpremultiplied, so the bridge highlight and eye-socket fades
   were translucent near-black: a grey-brown ring round both eyes and down the
   nose on all 17 portraits. Bridge/forehead luma ratio 0.81-0.91 before,
   1.005-1.05 after.
