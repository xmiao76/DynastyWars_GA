- Painted key art: a Five-Tigers homage over the burning Red-Cliffs fleet,
  generated with the grok provider. The GPT subscription quota was
  exhausted; the owner logged into Grok and asked for the retry there.
  The master is in art/ui-title/keyart-master.jpg (1248x832, sha256
  a5be2dca...8363). PROVENANCE.md records both hashes and the rebuild step.
- scripts/prepare-title-art.mjs does the 16:9 cover-crop, taken entirely
  off the bottom to remove the model's gibberish caption strip. It then
  downscales to 960x540 and bakes a bottom scrim, producing
  src/game/assets/title/keyart-backdrop.jpg (119 KB).
- BootScene preloads the backdrop as `ui:keyart-photo`. TitleScene prefers
  it and falls back to the procedural dusk battle (keyart.ts) when it is
  absent. The sprite hero lineup is gone because the heroes are in the
  painting.
- Baked gold-foil lockup with the bone 赤壁之战 subtitle. A new
  `ensureSheenStrip` (panels.ts) bakes 12 glint frames, luminance-weighted
  so the dark outline and shadow stay dark, and cycles them on the scene
  clock. There are no masks or WebGL-only blends, so both renderers draw
  it the same way.
- The bronze control plaque moved into the bottom scrim after the first
  capture showed it covering the three centre heroes' faces. The
  device-specific strings are byte-identical.
- The HI-SCORE line sits top centre, read from a guarded localStorage
  module (src/game/ui/hiscore.ts). GameOver and GameComplete write it.
- StyleFrameScene (?styleframe=1) was deleted as planned in UI-1.
