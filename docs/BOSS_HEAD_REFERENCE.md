# BODY-6 — nine boss facial reference briefs

## Authority and limits

Owner approved semi-realistic painted warriors (ART-18, 2026-09-23); this
brief extends that direction to the nine bosses. The novel is our legendary
design source, not forensic evidence. Original artwork only; no copied
Capcom sprites, TV actors, or modern costumes.

Only four bosses below have explicit physical descriptions in the sources.
The other five are interpretation from temperament, role, and stage
tradition, and the brief says so for each — no invented quotation.

Sources (accessed 2026-09-24):
- [Romance of the Three Kingdoms, chapter 1 (Cao Cao)](https://ctext.org/sanguo-yanyi/ch1/zhs)
- [Chapter 5 (Lu Bu at Hulao Pass)](https://ctext.org/sanguo-yanyi/ch5/zh)
- [Chapter 18 (Xiahou Dun's eye)](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E5%A4%8F%E4%BE%AF%E6%83%87/6766)
- [Records of the Three Kingdoms, Xu Chu: 長八尺餘，腰大十圍](https://www.shidianguji.com/zh/mid-page/7665463064125046810)

## Shared art direction

Same rules as `HERO_FACE_REFERENCE.md`: believable adult bone structure,
natural eyelids wrapping irises, lips integrated into skin, ears with
structure, light from upper left, restrained brushwork, purposeful
expressions. Boss heads must sit on the existing procedural necks without a
pastel seam: match the `ENEMY_FIGURES` complexion per boss (listed below)
within half a shade. Right-facing three-quarter gameplay heads plus bust
portraits; identity must agree with the in-play head at HUD size.

### Lu Bu / 呂布

- Explicit, chapter 5: “頭戴三叉束髮紫金冠，體掛西川紅錦百花袍，身披獸面吞頭連環鎧，腰繫勒甲玲瓏獅蠻帶” — plus the twin 雉尾 pheasant plumes of the stage tradition.
- Must read: the handsomest and most splendid warrior in the cast; youthful
  arrogant beauty, ornate purple-gold triple-prong crown with twin plumes.
- Interpretation: proud level gaze, faint smirk; flawless fair skin
  (0xf0c8a0); clean-shaven. Arrogance, not cruelty.
- Avoid: aged veteran, generic helmet, copying any actor's Lu Bu.

### Cao Cao / 曹操

- Explicit, chapter 1: “身長七尺，細眼長髯”.
- Must read: narrow long eyes (the narrowest aperture in the cast), long
  straight dark beard; slightly shorter build reads in-game already.
- Interpretation: cold watchful intelligence, thin pressed lips; sallow
  olive-tan skin (0xd8b48c); plain gold crown, black gauze cap wings.
  Suspicion without snarling — he schemes, he does not rage.
- Avoid: round staring eyes, fat jolly villain, horned helmet.

### Xiahou Dun / 夏侯惇

- Explicit, chapter 18: struck in the left eye by Cao Xing's arrow at
  Xiaopei, he pulled out the arrow WITH the eyeball and swallowed it —
  拔矢啖睛, “父精母血，不可棄也”.
- Must read: a one-eyed veteran — hollow scarred left socket with a
  diagonal scar crossing it; the remaining right eye fierce and glaring.
- Interpretation: hot temper barely held; weathered ruddy-tan skin
  (0xcfa87e); full dark beard; simple headband, armor plain and worn.
- Avoid: eyepatch pirate, symmetrical pretty face, bloody gore close-up.

### Xu Chu / 許褚

- Explicit, Records: “長八尺餘，腰大十圍” — over eight chi tall, ten
  wei around the waist; the novel keeps the blunt mass (虎痴, "tiger fool").
- Must read: massive blunt square head, heavy brow, small eyes set deep,
  thick neck wider than the jaw; the bulkiest head in the cast.
- Interpretation: dull honest menace, mouth slightly open; coarse ruddy
  skin (0xc08a68); short full beard; plain iron helm, no ornament.
- Avoid: handsome features, slender neck, cunning expression.

### Zhang Liao / 張遼

- No explicit physical description in the novel. What the sources give is
  presence: the general who broke Sun Quan's host at Hefei with 800 men,
  whom Cao Pi praised above all commanders of past and present.
- Interpretation (staged tradition + ART-17 `phoenix` precedent): mature
  awe-inspiring commander — long composed eyes, level heavy brows, full
  dark beard; tan skin (0xe0bc94); green-trimmed helm, cape.
- Avoid: pretty youth, wild glare, anything shared with Zhang He (they
  were once the same procedural face).

### Cao Ren / 曹仁

- No explicit physical description. What the sources give: Cao Cao's elder
  cousin, the veteran who held Fan against Guan Yu's flood.
- Interpretation: steadfast middle-aged defender — square calm face,
  even brows, full neat beard; warm tan skin (0xd4ac84); turban + plain
  steel-blue armor. Reliability made flesh.
- Avoid: decoration, rage, resemblance to Li Dian (clean-shaven) or
  Xiahou Dun (scarred).

### Xu Huang / 徐晃

- No explicit physical description. What the sources give: the disciplined
  veteran, Chang He's counterpart in steadiness, wielder of the great axe.
- Interpretation: severe sunken-cheeked discipline — hard slanted brows,
  deep-set eyes, long straight beard; weathered tan skin (0xd2a87c);
  plain headband, bronze-brown armor.
- Avoid: softness, youth, ornament.

### Zhang He / 張郃

- No explicit physical description. What the sources give: 郃識變數 — "He
  understood the shifts of battle", the shrewd tactician Zhuge Liang is
  said to have feared.
- Interpretation: the refined one — lean keen face, sharp intelligent eyes,
  clean-shaven (the deliberate contrast with bearded Zhang Liao); fair
  skin (0xe8c4a0); dark teal helm, cape.
- Avoid: beard of any kind, brutishness, resemblance to Li Dian.

### Li Dian / 李典

- No explicit physical description. What the sources give: the literate,
  reserved scholar-general, overshadowed at Hefei by Zhang Liao's glory.
- Interpretation: scholarly reserve — smooth youthful face, straight
  light brows, calm level eyes, clean-shaven; fair skin (0xe4c09a);
  dark blue helm, scholar's topknot visible at the nape.
- Avoid: age, scars, beard, fierceness of any kind.
