# ART-18 — five hero facial reference briefs

## Authority and limits

Owner approved semi-realistic painted warriors on 2026-09-23, and explicitly
chose novel-faithful date-red Wei Yan over the earlier tan complexion. The novel
is our legendary design source, not forensic evidence of these men's real faces.
Original artwork only; no copied Capcom sprites, TV actors, or modern costumes.

Sources (accessed 2026-09-23):
- [Romance of the Three Kingdoms, chapter 1](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/三國演義/第001回)
- [Chapter 7](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/三國演義/第007回)
- [Chapter 53](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/三國演義/第053回)
- [MOE dictionary, 目若朗星, quoting chapter 53](https://dict.revised.moe.edu.tw/dictView.jsp?ID=33056)

## Shared art direction

Believable adult human bone structure and soft tissue, natural eyelids wrapping
irises, lips integrated into surrounding skin, ears with structure, subtle skin
variation, facial hair growing from cheeks/chin rather than glued wedges. Light
from upper left, restrained brushwork and contrast, purposeful expressions.
Avoid thick cosmetic lip outlines, perfectly mirrored masks, huge glossy pupils,
identical noses, oversized hats, shiny plastic skin, and exaggerated caricature.
Retain readable silhouettes and familiar costume colors at small sizes. Crop
portraits as busts; gameplay views are right-facing three-quarter heads, not a
frontal portrait pasted onto a profile body. Keep identity across all expressions.

### Guan Yu / 关羽

- Explicit, chapter 1: “身长九尺，髯长二尺；面如重枣，唇若涂脂；丹凤眼，卧蚕眉”.
- Must read: mature imposing man, deep warm date-red skin; long narrow phoenix
  eyes, thick curved silkworm brows; naturally crimson lips, long dark beard.
- Interpretation: dignified reserved expression; strong elongated face and
  prominent cheek/jaw planes; dark green wrapped headcloth, forehead visible.
- Avoid: neon red face paint, lipstick outline, Egyptian-looking hood, thin
  angry V brows, triangular solid beard, obscured forehead/eyes.

### Zhang Fei / 张飞

- Explicit, chapter 1: “身长八尺，豹头环眼，燕颔虎须”.
- Must read: powerful broad head/jaw, intense round-open eyes within plausible
  sockets, coarse dense outward-bristling beard and moustache.
- Interpretation: deeply weathered dark-brown skin and black beard; dark/red
  head wrap; rugged intimidating middle-aged warrior. Dark face is a traditional
  visual convention, not a literal black-skin quotation from this passage.
- Avoid: bulging cartoon eyeballs, comic grin, perfectly toothed beard outline,
  black theatrical face paint, imitation of any actor.

### Zhao Yun / 赵云

- Explicit, chapter 7: “身长八尺，浓眉大眼，阔面重颐，威风凛凛”.
- Must read: thick brows, large attentive eyes, broad face and full strong chin.
- Interpretation: vigorous adult in his thirties, composed confidence, clean
  shaven, fair warm skin; practical silver helmet with red accent, blue collar.
  Exact age/complexion/shaving/helmet are art choices, not quoted requirements.
- Avoid: teenage doll, narrow pointed jaw, huge glossy eyes, makeup, saucer helmet.

### Huang Zhong / 黄忠

- Chapter 53: “虽今年近六旬，却有万夫不当之勇”; poem: “白发犹然困汉南”.
- Must read: around-sixty veteran, white hair, strong alert demeanor, natural
  weathering around eyelids/forehead/cheeks; not feeble or comically ancient.
- Interpretation: substantial white beard and moustache, sun-weathered warm skin,
  ochre/gold headband and clothing. White beard is coherent legendary iconography.
- Avoid: young face with white accessories, painted-on wrinkle grid, blank stare.

### Wei Yan / 魏延

- Explicit, chapter 53: “面如重枣，目若朗星”.
- Must read: deep reddish complexion, bright spirited eyes; clearly a different
  man from Guan Yu. This supersedes SKIN_WEIYAN's prior owner-selected tan.
- Interpretation: compact angular face, square chin, strong straight nose,
  forceful alert expression, short dark beard/moustache, dark purple head wrap.
  These structural and grooming details distinguish him without claiming textual
  authority. No literal skull deformity from the novel's “反骨” episode.
- Avoid: Guan Yu recolor, overly narrow sleepy eyes, old tan complexion retained
  in hands/neck, purple skin caused by global lighting.

## Review checklist (visual, not reducible to a color threshold)

1. Anatomically coherent adult face, including lid/iris/nose/mouth relationships.
2. Correct source-backed features, no costume covering defining features.
3. Individual silhouette, age, and expression rather than palette swaps.
4. Portrait and gameplay recognizable as the same character.
5. Consistent paint/light treatment and believable attachment to the rig.
6. Readable at real display size and in motion, not just enlarged assets.
7. Transparent edges, beard/hair layers, both facings, hurt/down not malformed.
8. Owner's visual objections remain actionable even if all technical gates pass.
