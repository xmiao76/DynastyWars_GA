# CMR authoring: bounded Acceptance-4 recovery

Owner authorization (2026-10-02): when the cast motion **A task** fails its fourth acceptance item (head-size / fully-observable weapon-length spread ≤10%; coherent direction ≤60°), the **same task** may automatically locate and repair its source drawings, redo only dependent preparation and measurement, and retry. No owner reset between eligible repair rounds. Use the standing [bounded task envelope](LOOP_AUTONOMY.md) for routine diagnosis and downstream close-out; no per-render/marker/record approval. This is an art/measurement recovery path, **not** an exemption from the three-total-failed-recovery-iterations rule in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md). It does not reopen an already BLOCKED task, override a provider failure, or authorize unrelated work.

## Result classification (never convert unknown into pass)

1. First validate that the measurement measures the right thing: fixed-scale original or displayed pixel crop; readable head versus helmet/hand; whole weapon tip-to-butt rather than only a bright paint highlight; foreshortened/occluded blades explicitly excluded from the comparable set. Discard invalid bounding-box, colour-cluster, thickness and mixed-pose proxy results. Recheck with an independent scale-controlled landmark or rotated stack. **Method invalid + no identified art defect = remeasure without an image request.** Do not redraw to placate a bad detector.
2. Compare **source** candidates, prepared frames and displayed pack at equal scale. Build an exact list of failing `sequence:index` drawings: actual oversized/undersized head or weapon, missing/occluded silhouette that prevents review, wrong design/direction, or registration mismatch. Reusing the same stable original throughout different poses is more important than forcing every foreshortened drawing to look side-on. Mark occluded but appropriate posing UNVERIFIED; if the remaining comparable sample cannot establish acceptance, take a targeted legibility repair only when the resulting pose stays natural.
3. If all comparable values pass but pose coverage is insufficient, gather better landmarks first. If genuine defects remain, use the bounded repair round below. If there is no evidence-based fix, STOP with exact uncertainty; thresholds are unchanged.

## Running pack projection (detect drift while budget remains)

Acceptance 4 is a FULL-pack gate but authoring runs sheet by sheet. Every past hero block (09A, 10A, 12A) passed sheets locally, then failed the pack with the budget spent. Close that trap with `scripts/pack-projection.mjs`:

- After the anchor (walk/locomotion) sheet is accepted, fix ONE global brief band from its median via `fixBand`. Every later sheet briefs against this band. Never silently re-center per sheet — 12A's per-sheet re-centered bands ratcheted the provider 191→300px. Changing the band later is an owner-approved re-brief with a recorded reason, enforced by `recordSheet`.
- After EACH sheet acceptance, record its scale-normalized measurements. On WARN, brief the next sheet inside the reported steering window. On STOP, repair or re-shoot an accepted sheet immediately — future sheets cannot repair extremes. On drift warning (monotonic sheet means), hold the band and re-brief the next sheet toward the anchor median.
- Commit `art/motion/<fighter>/pack-projection.json` (force-add like `registration.json`) so the trajectory is auditable. Spread is canonically (max−min)/mean; the module rejects any attempt to configure a different formula.

## Deterministic retouch before image rounds

The image provider's per-cell length scatter (~±15% even with labels) cannot reliably hit ±10%. CMR-11A proved the alternative: programmatic rigid-axis shaft extension/shortening (±1px achieved, seams reviewed) took FULL-56 from 31.3% to 9.1% with zero image requests. So: for length outliers on otherwise correct poses, attempt `scripts/motion-retouch.mjs` BEFORE reserve image rounds.

- Measure the shaft axis from enlarged crops, then `applyRetouchOp` (extend: slide cap + median-profile wood fill; shorten: forward overlap). Keep poses, identity and lighting untouched; only shaft length changes.
- Every op carries isolation, foreign-pixel, bounds and achieved-vs-predicted (±1px) gates; every file carries count/cell/edge gates via `verifySheetComposition`. Drivers must hash-pin inputs, stage all outputs, and write NOTHING unless every gate passes on every file (copy the atomic pattern, not the weiyan constants).
- Retouch is authored pixel repair, NOT an image-model edit and NOT a ledger event (authoring or repair). If the geometry forbids it (sheet edge, hand/tassel tangle, boot adjacency — proven, not assumed), fall through to reserve image rounds. Re-measure and re-run the projection after every retouch.

## Head gate: overlay landmarks first

Auto head/face detectors void too easily (costume-colour merges, pauldron/hand misfires, pose-confounded clusters) and a void must not auto-block an otherwise consistent set. Primary head evidence is the overlay-landmark protocol: place crown/chin landmarks on enlarged fixed-scale crops of comparable upright poses, verify each endpoint sits on drawn pixels, and gate the comparable set at ≤10% like weapons. Auto-detector output is supporting evidence only. A set with no obtainable landmarks stays UNVERIFIED (never a pass); acceptance then rests on the recorded visual-consistency evidence plus producer review, as in the 10A/11A precedent — with the same honest disclosure, not a silent pass.

## Reserve and retry: maximum three rounds per fighter

The original [authoring ledger](../art/motion/generation-budget.json) remains capped at 200 requests. A separate [repair ledger](../art/motion/acceptance4-repair-budget.json) authorizes **three additional focused requests per remaining fighter/horse** (17×3=51; combined cap 251). Spend image rounds LAST: run the projection gate during authoring, then deterministic retouch for length outliers, and only then reserve image rounds for geometry retouch cannot reach. Guan Yu's generation remains closed. The starting repair ledger is empty; historical soldier repairs already counted in the authoring ledger and are not charged again. Earlier work or a new task ID does not reset either ledger.

- One round = **one** image request, at most **nine named problem cells** in that request; multiple source sequences may share that one clearly labelled batch. A sequence can have at most two whole-sheet candidates under the original policy; these are focused repairs, **not** unlimited whole-sheet attempts.
- Use `remainingRepairRounds` and `reserveRepairRound` from `scripts/motion-acceptance4-repair.mjs` with source SHA-256, specific cells and located defect. Persist the returned state with `saveRepairLedger` **before** calling `image_generate`; do not spend before reserving. An outstanding `pending` reservation blocks another request. An interrupted or failed provider call is not automatically refunded.
- Use original fighter body/portrait reference(s) and the offending source image(s), selected with `read_image` first, with verified ordered hashes. Ask for *only* the named cells and explain anatomy/rigid weapon/consistent scale, pose, lighting and floor; do not substitute a new character identity. Preserve each output and prompt/provider evidence. Reject a malformed or drifting image even if a number improves.
- Record each outcome through `settleRepairRound`: `accepted` (with output SHA-256), `rejected` or `tool-failed`, plus a concrete reason. If a crash leaves `pending`, reconcile using the saved tool output/log, never blindly reserve another request or reset the ledger. Commit budget/provenance separately from large ignored diagnostics as part of the same selected task.
- An accepted source repair belongs in a **new source override or immutable before/after original pair**. Never overwrite an original without preserving it. Rebuild only affected registration/trim/pages/metadata, and revalidate the *full* pack. Measure untouched frame hashes to prove they did not change. Existing fighter budgets, page/decode ceilings and Guan Yu baseline remain in force.
- After each round, regenerate fixed-scale head review, full rigid weapon landmarks and an appropriate rotated shared-ruler overlay; inspect actual image pixels at gameplay and detail size. Capture numeric min/max/spread for a sufficiently diverse fully-observable comparable set, with support/evidence per endpoint. Re-run real action/transition captures when an exposed frame changed. **A smaller bounding box or thick shaft alone is not a passing weapon length.** Mark every remaining unknown/occluded sample, do not invent a measurement. Acceptance requires ≤10% head and observable length spreads, ≤60° visible step (or authored bridge), intact hands/weapon/body, ≤1.5 logical-unit ground error, and unchanged applicable timing.
- If ≤10% cannot be demonstrated with trustworthy endpoints after repair, retain IN_PROGRESS while the three allowed task iterations remain. At three total **failed recovery consumer iterations** or exhausted resources with no valid next step, park this task BLOCKED with a WAITING_FOR_OWNER disposition, exact evidence and one consolidated owner notice; continue independent ready work. Never globally pause merely to await a task's spending decision. A consumer iteration can contain one diagnosis/repair/remeasure round and a scoped commit; do not count every failed formula inside one diagnostic iteration as a new task iteration, and do not promise unlimited fresh loops.

The path may use existing `scripts/motion-pipeline.mjs` and the soldier repair example `scripts/splice-repairs.mjs` as references; they are **not** safe generic auto-splicers. A repair with a detached blade must establish hand-to-haft connectivity and pose-matched scale. Do not change gameplay, remove old art fallback, loosen verification, edit another fighter, or spawn a second writer/runner.

## Loop handoff

A consumer entry in [`.loop/loop.md`](../.loop/loop.md) invokes this protocol only for the selected CMR-A task. Within one iteration it diagnoses and advances the selected task; it never runs a second task or bypasses the top-of-queue order. The next producer independently reviews accepted after-images on fresh pixels. This protocol makes a *known solvable* art failure actionable, not guaranteed green. Status/result remain honest.
